# 🧠 Infinity Quest

> Turn notes into quizzes — instantly.

Infinity Quest is a smart and lightweight app that transforms your **text or PDF files into interactive MCQ (Multiple Choice Questions)**.
Perfect for students who want to revise faster, smarter, and more efficiently.

## ✨ Features

* 📄 **Text & PDF to MCQ**  
  Upload notes or paste text and generate questions instantly  

* 🧠 **Smart Question Generation**  
  Automatically creates multiple-choice questions for practice  

* 📊 **Custom Question Count**  
  Choose how many MCQs you want  

* 🎯 **Difficulty Selection**  
  Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed modes  

* 📱 **Clean & Focused UI**  
  Distraction-free learning experience  

* ⚡ **Fast Processing**  
  Generate quizzes within seconds  

---

## 🧩 How It Works

1. 📄 Upload a PDF or paste your text  
2. 🎚️ Select difficulty & number of questions  
3. ⚡ Click **Generate**  
4. 📝 Practice MCQs instantly
